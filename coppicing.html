Coppicing

cop·pic·ing

/ˈkɒpɪsɪŋ/ KOP-ih-sing

  1. The traditional woodland-management practice of cutting a tree down to a stump so that it sprouts new shoots from the stump or roots and ultimately regrows.

Source: Wikipedia, “Coppicing” (pronunciation and first definition).

The Wardens' duty

The Wardens of Oaksworn are charged to perform the Coppicing every season, removing members who are inactive or who have not grown roots in the community. The periodic thinning of our ranks helps the grove regrow healthier.

Anyone thinned from our ranks as a part of the coppicing is always welcome to rejoin.
