The Coppicing

What it means

Coppicing is the old woodland practice of cutting a tree back to its base so it regrows stronger. Thinned groves regrow healthier.

The Wardens' duty

Once per month, the Wardens are required to perform the Coppicing: removing members who are inactive, or who have not grown roots in the community.

Anyone thinned from our ranks as a part of the coppicing is always welcome to rejoin.
