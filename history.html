History

The History of the Oaksworn

  • Fall 2026 — Oaksworn forms an Alliance guild in World of Warcraft: Forever, built around open-world PvP.
  • Winter 2023 - Summer 2026 — Oaksworn bounces around a variety of games together, including WoW (retail), DAoC (Eden), Rust: Awakening, Smite 2, Dune: Awakening, Arc Raiders, Fortnite, Diablo IV, Path of Exile II, Last Epoch, Ashes of Creation, Sea of Thieves, Guild Wars II, and Albion Online.
  • May 2022 — Oaksworn reactivates Emerald Dream (Alliance) guild on WoW (Shadowlands).
  • April 2022 — Oaksworn begins to mix in other games -- Smite, Final Fantasy, Deep Rock Galactic -- more frequently.
  • February 2022 — Oaksworn forms on the first day of Lost Ark early access (NA-East, Karta).
  • December 2021 — Oaksworn forms, in part, on Elder Scrolls Online under the Ebonheart Pact banner (NA-PC).
  • November 2021 — Members explore various games (Age of Empires, Deep Rock Galactic, Final Fantasy, etc.).
  • October 2021 — New World implodes.
  • September 2021 — Oaksworn forms in New World (Ydalir - NA East).
  • July 2021 — Oaksworn forms in New World (closed beta).
  • June 2021 — Oaksworn forms in Crowfall.
  • May 2021 — Oaksworn takes a break from WoW (Shadowlands) and shifts to Sea of Thieves and Deep Rock Galactic.
  • March 2020 — Oaksworn reforms in WoW (Shadowlands).
  • January 2019 — Oaksworn forms up in Phoenix (DAoC free shard).
  • August 2018 — Oaksworn returns to WoW (BFA).
  • April 2017 — Oaksworn moves to Overwatch.
  • December 2016 — Oaksworn forms up in WoW (Legion).
  • May 2016 — Oaksworn officially forms in Uthgard Beta.
  • December 2015 — Oaksworn reforms as a multi-game community.
  • 2002-2012 — Oaksworn exists as an informal crew of RL friends playing DAoC, WoW, GW2, FFXIV, and other MMORPGs.*

* In some early games, the Oaksworn also went by the name "Stormur Vakten".
