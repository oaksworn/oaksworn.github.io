Our History

  1. Fall 2026

    Oaksworn forms an Alliance guild in World of Warcraft: Forever, built around open-world PvP.

    WoW: Forever

  2. Winter 2023 – Summer 2026

    Oaksworn bounces around a variety of games together.

    WoW (retail)DAoC (Eden)RustSmite 2Dune: AwakeningArc RaidersFortniteDiablo IVPath of Exile IILast EpochAshes of CreationSea of ThievesGuild Wars IIAlbion Online

    Oaksworn reactivates the Emerald Dream (Alliance) guild on WoW (Shadowlands).

    WoW (Shadowlands)

  4. April 2022

    Oaksworn begins to mix in other games more frequently.

    SmiteFinal FantasyDeep Rock Galactic

  5. February 2022

    Oaksworn forms on the first day of Lost Ark early access (NA-East, Karta).

    Lost Ark

  6. December 2021

    Oaksworn forms, in part, on Elder Scrolls Online under the Ebonheart Pact banner (NA-PC).

    Elder Scrolls Online

  7. November 2021

    Members explore various games.

    Age of EmpiresDeep Rock GalacticFinal Fantasyand more

  8. October 2021

    New World implodes.

    New World

  9. September 2021

    Oaksworn forms in New World (Ydalir, NA East).

    New World

  10. July 2021

    Oaksworn forms in New World (closed beta).

    New World

  11. June 2021

    Oaksworn forms in Crowfall.

    Crowfall

  12. May 2021

    Oaksworn takes a break from WoW (Shadowlands) and shifts to other games.

    Sea of ThievesDeep Rock Galactic

  13. March 2020

    Oaksworn reforms in WoW (Shadowlands).

    WoW (Shadowlands)

  14. January 2019

    Oaksworn forms up in Phoenix (DAoC free shard).

    DAoC

  15. August 2018

    Oaksworn returns to WoW (BFA).

    WoW (BFA)

  16. April 2017

    Oaksworn moves to Overwatch.

    Overwatch

  17. December 2016

    Oaksworn forms up in WoW (Legion).

    WoW (Legion)

  18. May 2016

    Oaksworn officially forms in Uthgard Beta.

    DAoC (Uthgard)

  19. December 2015

    Oaksworn reforms as a multi-game community.

    Multi-game

  20. 2002-2012

    Oaksworn exists as an informal crew of real-life friends playing Dark Age of Camelot and other MMORPGs.*

    DAoCWoWGW2FFXIVand others
