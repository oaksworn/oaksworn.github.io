Our Vibe

Oaksworn is a laid-back band of friends who like open-world PvP, battlegrounds, and the immersive feel of the Alliance. We take the fight seriously and ourselves lightly.

We don't do drama, tryhard toxicity, or mandatory anything. Play when you can, skip it when you can't, and no one will make you feel bad about either.

When We Play

Most of our group play is in the evening, typically 8-11pm Eastern (7-10pm Central).

Our members are mostly in the Central and Eastern time zones. Nothing is required, so if you play at other hours you will still find a home here.
