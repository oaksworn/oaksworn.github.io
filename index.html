Who We Are

Oaksworn is a chill, casual U.S. guild.

Our focus is on open-world PvP, battlegrounds, dungeons, and immersive in-game events.

When We Play

8-11pm Eastern (7-10pm Central).

Our members are mostly US Central and Eastern time zones.
