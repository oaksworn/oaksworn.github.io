Expectations

  1. Defend the Alliance.
    • At end game, we'll do a lot of open world PvP and BGs.
    • All Horde are kill on sight.
  2. Stay immersed.
    • Join thematic events to improve the Oaksworn renown.
    • Voice chat will never be required of any member. Communicate in-game only.
    • We respect roleplayers, but we are not a RP guild.
  3. Keep it chill and casual.
    • No toxic tryhards.
    • No obnoxious or offensive names/communication.
    • No required play times or activities. We don't pay your sub.
  4. Coordinate effectively.
    • Use the in-game ping system effectively.
    • Set a common focus and assist on the same enemy target.
    • We encourage members to install a few helpful addons (e.g., Overlord Forever, OneForAll Forever, BlitzFramesTargets).
  5. Choose your division (see ranks tab).
    • Want to join our main fighting force? Join the ranks of our Grove Protectors.
    • Want to wage a stealth, guerilla war against the Horde? Join the ranks of our Nightshades.
    • Prefer to level, gather, craft, and explore? Join the ranks of our Verdant Expedition.
