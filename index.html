Who we are

Oaksworn is a friendly Alliance guild built for open-world PvP and good company. We take the fights seriously and ourselves lightly, with a touch of fantasy flavor and none of the hardcore roleplay.

Our endgame is the open world: holding fronts, defending keeps, and ambushing the other side on the road.

World PvPThemed eventsNo voice chatDrama free

The Oath

  • No jerks. Treat everyone, friend or foe, decently.
  • No obnoxious names. Fantasy is welcome, trolling is not.
  • Text only. Voice chat is never required.
  • Show up when you can. Nobody takes attendance.
  • Settle disputes privately with an officer.

The Campaign

We fight for control of the open world with the Overlord Forever addon, which every member installs.

  • Weekly front pushes
  • Keep and outpost defense
  • Monthly themed nights
  • Occasional roleplay moots, always optional

Join the grove

New to the guild or just curious? Whisper any Warden, Elder, or Grove Protector in game, or ask in guild chat. Tell us your class, how you like to play, and when you are usually online.

Install the Overlord Forever addon before your first push, and you are ready.
