Alliance Guild (RP-PvP) · World of Warcraft: Forever

Oaksworn

Joining the Oaksworn comes with the following expectations:

  • Defend the Alliance and vanquish the Horde.
  • Stay immersed. Voice chat will never be required of any member.
  • Join thematic events to improve the Oaksworn renown (thematic vibes -- not hardcore RPing).
  • Keep it chill and casual. No toxic tryhards, no obnoxious or offensive names/communication.
  • Coordinate effectively. Use in-game ping and install a few helpful addons (e.g., Overlord Forever, OneForAll Forever, BlitzFramesTargets).

Ranks

The Wardens

The Oaksworn leadership. Years of experience together required.

The Grove Protectors

The vanguard of the Oaksworn defenses. This is our primary fighting force, tasked with offensive maneuvers and hold keeps.

The Nightshades

The guerilla division of Oaksworn forces. Espionage, scouting, ambush, and sabotage. (Stealth-only unit, restricted to rogues, druids, and Night Elves.)

The Verdant Expedition

The exploration and commercial unit. Those tasked with building the treasury of the Oaksworn (PvE-focused players).

Initiates

New recruits that have not yet sworn their full oaths.
