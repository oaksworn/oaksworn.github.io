The Oath

  1. I will fight for the Alliance.
    • Conquer and defend open world PvP objectives; win honor in battlegrounds.
    • The Overlord Forever addon is required for all members.
    • Secure treasure to aid the Alliance war effort.
  2. I will stay true to this world.
    • Immersion-breaking voice chat will never be required of any member.
    • Join thematic events to improve the Oaksworn renown.
    • We respect roleplayers but should not that we are not a strict-RP guild.
  3. I will fly the Oaksworn colors.
    • Wear the guild emblem whenever your gear and transmog allow it.
    • By wearing the Oaksworn colors, you strengthen our group identity.
  4. I will keep my head.
    • Stay chill and casual.
    • No obnoxious or offensive names/communication. No toxic tryhards.
    • No required play times or activities. We don't pay your sub.
  5. I will train to secure victory.
    • No voice chat means we need careful in-game coordination.
    • Hotkey that in-game ping system and set a common focus to assist on enemy targets.
    • Consider some other helpful addons (OneForAll Forever, BlitzFramesTargets).
  6. I will specialize to advance the Oaksworn cause.
    • Want to join our main fighting force? Join the ranks of our Grove Protectors.
    • Want to wage a stealth, guerilla war against the Horde? Join the ranks of our Nightshades.
    • Prefer to level, gather, craft, and explore? Join the ranks of our Verdant Expedition.
