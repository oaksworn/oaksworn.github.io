The Oath

  1. NOTE: The ForeverGuild+ and Overlord Forever addons are required for all members to facilite in-game coordination without voice chat.
    • I will defend our people from the Horde.
      • Conquer and hold open world PvP objectives
      • Win honor in battlegrounds.
      • Secure treasure to aid the Alliance war effort.
    • I will stay true to this world.
      • Immersion-breaking voice chat will never be required of any member.
      • Join thematic events to improve the Oaksworn renown.
      • Respect roleplayers -- but note that this is NOT a strict-RP guild.
    • I will duly represent the Oaksworn colors.
      • Wear the guild emblem whenever your gear and transmog allow it.
      • Whenever possible, work to strengthen our reputation and presence on the server.
      • Bringing shame or dishonor to the Oaksworn will likely lead to removal at the next coppicing, if not sooner.
    • I will keep my head in the choas of battle.
      • Stay chill, keep it casual.
      • No obnoxious or offensive names/communication. No toxic tryhards.
      • No required play times or activities. We don't pay your sub.
    • I will prepare and train diligintly to secure victory for the Alliance.
      • No voice chat means we need careful in-game coordination.
      • Hotkey that in-game ping system and set a common focus to assist on enemy targets.
      • Consider some other helpful (optional) addons (OneForAll Forever, BlitzFramesTargets).
    • I will specialize to advance the Oaksworn cause.
      • Want to join our main fighting force? Join the ranks of our Grove Protectors.
      • Want to wage a stealth, guerilla war against the Horde? Join the ranks of our Nightshades.
      • Prefer to level, gather, craft, and explore? Join the ranks of our Verdant Expedition.
