Ranks

The Wardens

The Oaksworn leadership. Required to lead events, recruit members, and perform the coppicing.

The Grove Protectors

The vanguard of the Oaksworn defenses. This is our primary fighting force, tasked with offensive maneuvers and holding keeps.

The Nightshades

The guerrilla division of Oaksworn forces. Espionage, scouting, ambush, and sabotage. (Stealth-only unit, restricted to rogues, druids, and Night Elves.)

The Verdant Expedition

The exploration and commercial unit. Those tasked with building the treasury of the Oaksworn (PvE-focused players).

Initiates

New recruits that have not yet sworn their full oaths.

Any group of Oaksworn may petition the Wardens to begin their own thematic, named unit within our ranks.
