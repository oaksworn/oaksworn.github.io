Ranks and Divisions

The Wardens

The Oaksworn leadership.

Required to:

  • Lead events
  • Recruit members
  • Perform the coppicing

The Grove Protectors

The vanguard of the Oaksworn defenses.

  • Our primary fighting force
  • Offensive maneuvers
  • Holding keeps

The Nightshades

The guerrilla division of Oaksworn forces.

  • Espionage and scouting
  • Ambush and sabotage

Stealth-only unit, restricted to rogues, druids, and Night Elves.

The Verdant Expedition

The exploration and commercial unit.

  • Building the treasury of the Oaksworn
  • PvE-focused players

Initiates

New recruits that have not yet sworn their full oaths.

Any group of Oaksworn may petition the Wardens to begin their own thematic, named unit within our ranks.
