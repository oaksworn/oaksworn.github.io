Ranks and Divisions

The Wardens

The Oaksworn leadership. Responsible for guild strategyt, event leadership, recruitment, and the seasonal coppicing.

The Grove Protectors

The Oaksworn vanguard in its war against the Horde.

  • Our primary fighting force.
  • Specialization in taking open-world objectives and defending Alliance cities

The Nightshades

The guerrilla warfare division of the Oaksworn

  • Espionage, scouting, ambush, and sabotage.
  • Stealth-only: restricted to rogues, druids, and Night Elves

The Verdant Expedition

Reserve unit committed to exploration and commercial activities.

  • Dedicated to building the treasury of the Oaksworn
  • Reserved for alts and purely PvE-focused players

Initiates

New recruits that have not yet sworn their full oaths.

Any group of Oaksworn may petition the Wardens to begin their own thematic, named unit within our ranks.
